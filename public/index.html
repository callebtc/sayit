The lost art of being read to

              

                
Remember being read to as a child? Someone else carried the words, and all you had to do was listen. Somewhere along the way, reading turned into work: endless tabs, long emails, articles saved for later.

                
Say It gives that feeling back. Select any text, press a shortcut, and a natural voice reads it aloud, right on your Mac. So lean back, close your eyes, and let your Mac do the reading.
